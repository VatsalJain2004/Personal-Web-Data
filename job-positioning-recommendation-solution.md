## 3. Your lack of Flutter projects isn't actually a major problem

This is where I think you're being unnecessarily hard on yourself.

For an experienced developer, there are two kinds of evidence:

Projects and professional experience.

Professional experience is generally stronger.

If you say:

“I built a Flutter weather app.”

That's a project.

If you say:

“At Kody Technolab, I owned end-to-end development of 50+ Flutter screens and reduced video startup latency by 83%.”

That's professional engineering experience.

The second one is much more valuable.

A recruiter doesn't normally say:

“Interesting, but where is his Todo app?”

when they see that you actually worked on a professional Flutter application.

Your problem is therefore not lack of Flutter projects.

It's that your résumé currently has a somewhat confusing identity:

Flutter Developer + Backend Developer + React + Django + Python + Full Stack + DSA + Open Source.

A recruiter looking for one particular role may not immediately know what you are.

## 13. And one very important correction to your job-search strategy

You said you've been applying for roughly two months and getting almost no response.

I wouldn't conclude from that that you're not employable.

It may mean your application mechanism is bad.

If you're doing:

Job portal → Apply → Upload same résumé → Wait

you're essentially entering the highest-volume part of the hiring funnel.

Your new strategy should be:

Find company → identify relevant opening → tailor résumé → apply on company site → identify recruiter/founder/engineering manager → send concise message → follow up.

And for startups:

Find company → inspect tech stack → identify engineering need → apply → contact founder/engineering lead.

That's where your profile becomes much more interesting.
